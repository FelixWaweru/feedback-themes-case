# Kvist Bank theme set

Three-tier hierarchy used by the extraction pipeline. Every assignment must be
exactly one path from this set. Names are globally unique at each midlevel and
specific tier so the flat projection forms a consistent tree.

_Version: 1.0_

## Customer Service & Advisory

How customers experience reaching, interacting with, and being followed up by support staff and advisors across all contact channels.

### Support responsiveness & follow-up

How quickly, reliably, and completely support answers customer inquiries and honors promised follow-ups.

- **Reply speed across contact channels** — How quickly the bank replies to emails, calls, and chat messages, from answers within minutes to many days of silence.
- **Callback and follow-up reliability** — Whether promised callbacks, status follow-ups, and agreed next steps actually happen or never materialize.
- **Answer accuracy and completeness** — Whether responses are correct, complete, and consistent, versus half-answers, wrong information, or cases left open.

### Access to support

How easy it is for customers to reach a human who can help when they need assistance.

- **Reaching support and waiting times** — Ease of getting through to support, including phone queues, chat availability outside office hours, and general accessibility.
- **Chatbot support and escalation to human staff** — How well the chatbot resolves issues and whether customers can reach a human when the chatbot falls short.

### Advisor quality & relationship continuity

The competence, conduct, and continuity of advisors and contact persons over the course of the relationship.

- **Advisor competence and business understanding** — Whether advisors know the rules, understand the customer's business, and give tailored, solution-oriented advice.
- **Contact person continuity and handover quality** — Whether customers keep the same contact person over time or face advisor turnover, repeated information requests, and poor handovers.
- **Professionalism and advisory orientation of staff** — Whether interactions feel friendly, proactive, and genuinely advisory rather than pushy or sales-driven.

## Pricing & Contract Terms

How customers perceive the level, transparency, and predictability of rates, fees, and contractual conditions.

### Interest rates & total cost

Perceived level, competitiveness, and explanation of interest rates and the overall price of financing.

- **Rate level and competitiveness** — How high or competitive business credit and savings rates are perceived, including comparisons with competitors.
- **Advertised vs actual rates and pricing** — Whether quoted or advertised 'from' rates and calculator figures match the final offer and agreement.
- **Clarity of interest calculation and rate explanations** — Whether interest calculation, effective rates, and total financing costs are clearly explained.

### Fees & charges

The size, clarity, and predictability of fees charged on accounts and financing.

- **Fee level and structure clarity** — The size, number, and clarity of establishment, instalment, administration, and recurring monthly fees.
- **Fee predictability and disputed charges** — Whether fees arrive as expected or appear as hidden, unexpected, or duplicate charges that customers dispute.

### Contract terms & changes

Transparency and fairness of binding periods, exit conditions, and changes to rates or credit terms.

- **Lock-in periods and termination costs** — Experiences with binding periods, notice of lock-in terms, break fees, and obstacles when ending an agreement early.
- **Notice of rate and credit-term changes** — Whether rate increases and changes to credit terms are announced with reasonable notice and explanation or made without warning.

## Digital Experience & Technology

How well the bank's digital channels — portal, app, login, and integrations — work for everyday business banking.

### Platform reliability & access

Stability, speed, and availability of the portal, mobile app, and login.

- **Portal and app stability, speed, and uptime** — Whether the portal and app run fast and reliably, covering crashes, session timeouts, lost form data, portal hangs, and how quickly reported bugs are fixed.
- **Login and BankID reliability** — Whether logging in, including with BankID, works smoothly or fails.
- **Mobile app availability** — Whether a mobile app exists and offers sufficient functionality, including applying or managing services from mobile.

### Self-service functionality & data

Whether customers can perform everyday tasks themselves in the portal and trust the data shown.

- **Everyday self-service tasks in the portal** — Ease of finding functions and handling routine tasks such as changing account numbers, terminating credit lines, or uploading documents.
- **Balance and statement accuracy** — Whether displayed balances, statements, and invoice data are correct and trustworthy.
- **Data export and reporting** — Ability to download statements, CSV files, and invoice data for accounting and reporting.
- **User and accountant access management** — Granularity of user access controls and ease of giving accountants or employees access.

### Integrations & automation

How well the bank connects with external accounting systems and automates routine financial flows.

- **Accounting software integration reliability** — Whether integrations with accounting systems such as Bokfør work reliably or fail.
- **Automation of invoice posting and payments** — Degree of automation in invoice posting, monthly direct debits, and other routine payments without manual effort.

## Speed & Process Efficiency

How quickly and smoothly applications, credit decisions, payouts, and routine changes are completed.

### Application & onboarding

Ease and speed of applying and getting started with the bank.

- **Application ease and simplicity** — How simple and clear the application and setup process is, including digital signing and mobile application.
- **Documentation requirements and automated data retrieval** — The volume of paperwork demanded versus automatic retrieval of figures and smooth document handling.

### Credit decisions & case processing

Turnaround time for credit assessments, approvals, and ongoing case handling.

- **Credit assessment and approval speed** — How fast credit decisions are made, from instant weekend approvals to multi-week rejections.
- **Processing speed vs advertised promises** — How actual processing times compare with marketing promises such as 'same-day' handling.
- **Routine change and case processing time** — How quickly routine changes, such as account number updates, and open cases are completed.

### Payout & funding speed

How quickly approved funds reach the customer's account.

- **Time from approval to payout** — How quickly loan and credit payouts reach the customer's account after approval.
- **Invoice financing payout speed and liquidity effect** — How fast funds are paid out after an invoice is submitted or sold, and the resulting effect on liquidity, payroll, and day-to-day operations.

## Communication, Transparency & Trust

Whether the bank communicates proactively and clearly, and keeps the commitments it makes.

### Proactive communication & status updates

Whether customers are kept informed without having to chase the bank.

- **Status updates during processing** — Whether customers receive timely updates on application and case status instead of silence.
- **Proactive outreach and information sharing** — Whether the bank and advisors reach out proactively with relevant information and follow-ups.

### Clarity of terms & decisions

How clearly and consistently terms, agreements, and decisions are communicated.

- **Clarity and consistency of terms and agreements** — Whether interest rates, agreement terms, and conditions are communicated clearly, consistently, and without contradictions.
- **Explanation and justification of credit decisions** — Whether approvals and rejections come with understandable reasons, including openness about credit assessments.

### Honesty & commitment follow-through

Whether the bank stands by what it promises in writing, verbally, and in marketing.

- **Honoring written and verbal commitments** — Whether the bank stands by written or verbal credit commitments and promises made during the process.
- **Accuracy of marketing and eligibility expectations** — Whether marketing sets realistic expectations about who qualifies and what is offered, versus attracting applicants who are then declined.

## Product Fit, Flexibility & Eligibility

Whether the bank's credit products, terms, and criteria genuinely fit the needs of small businesses.

### Product fit for small businesses

How well products, limits, and configurations match the size and industry of the business.

- **Product usefulness for business size and industry** — Whether overdraft, invoice financing, tax loans, and other products genuinely serve the cash-flow needs of different business sizes and industries.
- **Credit limits and facility configuration options** — Whether credit limits, loan amount caps, and facility configurations such as multiple overdrafts fit the company's needs.
- **Treatment of micro-companies relative to larger clients** — Whether micro-businesses are taken as seriously as larger clients.

### Repayment flexibility & hardship support

The bank's willingness to adapt repayment when business conditions change.

- **Payment deferrals and adjusted repayment plans** — Willingness to grant deferrals and adjust repayment schedules when businesses face difficulties.
- **Season-adapted repayment structures** — Whether repayment plans can be adapted to seasonal fluctuations in the business.

### Eligibility & credit criteria

The rules determining who qualifies for financing and on what terms.

- **Qualification and creditworthiness criteria** — Who qualifies for financing and credit lines, including rejections based on credit scores or business type.
- **Invoice acceptance rules** — Which invoices are accepted for financing, including rules on end-customer creditworthiness and B2C sales.
- **Credit limit changes at renewal** — Whether credit limits are maintained, reduced, or withdrawn at renewal.

## Billing & Account Administration

Correctness of billing and the handling of account closure and related administration.

### Billing accuracy

Whether invoices and charges issued to customers are correct.

- **Billing and invoice accuracy** — Correctness of invoices, including duplicate charges, wrong amounts, wrong due dates, and surprise invoice items.

### Account closure & exit administration

How smoothly account termination and related administration are handled.

- **Account termination process and post-closure billing** — The effort required to close an account and whether billing stops correctly afterwards, including delayed terminations and post-closure invoices.
