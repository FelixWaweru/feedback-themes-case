# Kvist Bank theme set

Three-tier hierarchy used by the extraction pipeline. Every assignment must be
exactly one path from this set. Names are globally unique at each midlevel and
specific tier so the flat projection forms a consistent tree.

_Version: merged-v1_

## Pricing & Fees

Clarity, fairness, and competitiveness of all fees, rates, and price changes across the customer lifecycle.

### Fee & rate transparency

Disclosure, justification, and communication of fees, rates, and price changes.

- **Unexplained or undisclosed fees** — Presence, clarity, and justification of establishment, administration, instalment, and other fees tied to invoice financing and account use.
- **Quoted vs effective rate consistency** — Whether quoted, displayed, or calculator-based rates match the effective interest costs customers ultimately face.
- **Fee complexity and invoice accuracy** — Clarity of fee structures and correctness of fee invoicing in day-to-day operations.
- **Rate-change notice on existing facilities** — Whether and how far in advance customers are informed of interest-rate changes on existing facilities.

### Pricing competitiveness

How customers perceive total cost relative to alternatives and competitors.

- **Rate vs fee trade-off vs competitors** — Overall value perception weighing interest rates against add-on fees compared to alternative financing providers.

## Digital Experience & Platform

Usability, reliability, feature breadth, and integration depth of the online portal, mobile app, and self-service tools.

### Portal usability & performance

Speed, stability, and feature completeness of the online banking portal and mobile app.

- **Portal speed and reliability** — Portal responsiveness, uptime, and freedom from crashes or incorrect balance displays.
- **Portal feature completeness** — Breadth of features available in the online portal and mobile app, including gaps in app scope versus web.
- **Login friction and access issues** — Ease of login, authentication friction, and access to the digital channel.

### Self-service & digital resolution

Whether digital channels can resolve issues independently and at all hours.

- **Chatbot and portal self-service capability** — Effectiveness of chatbot, app, and portal self-service tools in resolving customer issues without human escalation.

### Integrations & access control

Reliability and depth of third-party connections and access controls within digital tools.

- **Accounting software connectivity** — Reliability and setup ease of connections to accounting platforms such as Bokfør, including automated invoice flow.
- **User permission and role-based access** — Granularity of role-based access control and accountant access within the bank's digital tools.
- **Mobile app availability and scope** — Availability and functional scope of the mobile app relative to the web portal.

## Customer Support & Relationship

Speed, accessibility, expertise, and continuity of customer-facing staff and support channels.

### Support response & accessibility

Speed and ease of reaching human help across phone, email, and chat channels.

- **Support reply speed across channels** — Timeliness and reliability of replies to customer inquiries across email, chat, and case-management systems.
- **Phone queue wait and reachability** — Phone queue lengths, callback reliability, and ease of reaching a human agent.

### Staff helpfulness & advisory quality

Quality of advice, problem-solving attitude, and consistency of answers across staff.

- **Advisor solution-orientation** — Solution-orientation, expertise, and willingness of advisors to help solve customer problems.
- **Staff tone and reply consistency** — Tone, accuracy, and consistency of staff responses across phone, email, and chat channels.

### Advisor continuity & case handover

Continuity of named advisor relationships and case handover quality.

- **Advisor availability and case handover** — Availability of a named advisor and whether case handovers cause repeated information requests or restarts.
- **Staff turnover impact on commitments** — Impact of staff turnover on customer commitments, relationship continuity, and case progress.
- **Advisor proactive contact and follow-through** — Advisor familiarity with the customer's business, proactive outreach, and follow-through after initial contact.

## Service Delivery & Processing

Speed, reliability, and transparency of application processing, credit decisions, and fund disbursement.

### Application & decision turnaround

Time and clarity from application submission through credit decision to funding.

- **Processing time vs promised timeline** — How long application processing and credit decisions take compared to advertised or promised timelines.
- **Approval and rejection turnaround time** — Speed and transparency of both rapid approvals and prolonged or unclear rejections, including for start-ups.
- **Application processing speed** — Overall speed of the application-to-funding pipeline from the customer's perspective.
- **Document requirements and burden** — Volume and appropriateness of document requests during onboarding and ongoing use.

### Payout & disbursement

Speed and reliability of fund disbursement once approved.

- **Invoice-to-payout turnaround** — Time elapsed from invoice submission or approval to funds reaching the customer's account.
- **Disbursement reliability** — Consistency and reliability of disbursement timing, from same-day to multi-day delays.

### Account administrative changes

Speed and reliability of administrative changes to existing accounts.

- **Account administrative change processing** — Processing speed and reliability for updates, amendments, and administrative changes on active accounts.

## Credit & Repayment Management

How credit limits are set, communicated, and changed, and how repayment and hardship are handled.

### Credit assessment & limit decisions

Credit-limit decisions, eligibility criteria, and lifecycle changes to facilities.

- **Credit limit transparency and eligibility criteria** — How credit limits are set, communicated, and changed, including eligibility filters and renewal reductions.
- **Unannounced credit line and fee adjustments** — Unannounced or poorly communicated changes to credit limits, fees, and credit-check frequency during the customer lifecycle.

### Repayment flexibility & hardship

Flexibility in repayment timing, deferrals, and seasonal adjustments.

- **Repayment plan adaptation and deferrals** — How the bank accommodates repayment timing, deferrals, seasonal adjustments, and credit-line changes without full reapplication.

### Late payment & collections

Handling of missed payments, collections, and payment-plan arrangements.

- **Reminder fees and payment-plan availability** — How the bank handles missed payments, including reminder fees, collections notices, and willingness to arrange payment plans.

## Information & Transparency

Consistency, clarity, and accessibility of information, terms, and records provided to customers.

### Cross-channel information alignment

Whether information is consistent across website, chatbot, advisors, and marketing materials.

- **Website vs advisor discrepancies** — Whether terms, pricing, and procedures are consistent across website, chatbot, and human advisors.

### Decision & term transparency

Clarity and honesty of decisions, terms, and marketing claims communicated to customers.

- **Assessment rationale and marketing-claim accuracy** — Clarity and honesty of marketing claims, eligibility criteria, rejection reasons, and post-signing term changes.
- **Status updates and term transparency** — Clarity and timeliness of status updates, eligibility criteria, product terms, and interest calculations communicated during the customer journey.

### Documentation & records access

Availability of historical records, statements, and exportable data.

- **Account statement and data export availability** — Customers' ability to obtain account statements, historical records, and exportable data files from the bank.
- **Document request appropriateness** — Appropriateness and clarity of document requests during onboarding and ongoing use.

## Account Lifecycle & Operations

Correctness of day-to-day operations and ease of account closure or exit.

### Account closure & exit

Steps, timelines, and obstacles when closing accounts or exiting contracts.

- **Closure friction and post-closure billing** — Friction, timelines, residual invoicing, and break fees customers face when attempting to close accounts or exit contracts.

### Billing & account accuracy

Correctness of invoices, balances, and customer data in routine operations.

- **Invoice addressing and balance display** — Correctness of invoices, account balances, and customer address data in day-to-day operations.

## Product Fit & Sales Practices

Whether financing products fit diverse business needs and how the bank approaches outbound selling.

### Product suitability & flexibility

Fit of loan amounts, binding periods, and facility structures to varying business profiles.

- **Loan limits, binding periods, and seasonal adaptation** — Whether loan amounts, binding periods, and facility structures fit companies of varying sizes and seasonal patterns.

### Sales practices

How the bank approaches outbound selling and whether customers feel pushed unneeded products.

- **Unsolicited product selling** — Whether outbound selling approaches feel appropriate or push products customers do not need.
